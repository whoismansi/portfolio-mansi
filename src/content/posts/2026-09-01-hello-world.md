This is my first post, I've been meaning to start this for a while.

My day job sits at the intersection of backend engineering and cloud infrastructure. Mostly Kubernetes and service mesh work, and all the small rituals that come with keeping production systems healthy. Twitter's too short for most of the technical stuff I want to write about, and Medium never really felt like mine. So I built this.

## Why a blog?

A lot of what I run into at work doesn't fit anywhere else. Distributed systems patterns I've had to learn the hard way, bits of infrastructure lore that usually only live in Slack DMs, the occasional thing I broke and had to reason through. Some of it feels worth sharing.

## What I'll be writing about

A rough sense of the mix:

- Postmortems from real incidents I've worked through, anonymized where they need to be
- Deep dives on distributed systems patterns and how they play out in production
- Short notes on tools I'm using, like Kubernetes operators, service meshes, and observability stacks
- Occasional non-technical writing when there's something on my mind I want to think out loud about

## What to expect

I'm not sure yet. I'll write when I have something to say. Posts might get updated as I learn more, and some might sit unfinished for a while. That's fine.
